Google Workspace Games

Clear your Gmail by playing a game. No install, no sign-in: drag a button to your bookmarks bar, open Gmail, and click it.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl + Shift + B (⌘ + Shift + B on a Mac).
  2. Drag a game button below onto the bar.
  3. Open your Gmail inbox and click the bookmark.

Your inbox is a brick wall: each brick is an email, named by its sender. Smash promotions and updates. People are steel bricks, scattered through the wall: they never break, and their mail is never touched. Pick a pace: the ball keeps speeding up until its top speed. Clear a wall and the next one is built from the rest of your mail. Catch drops for multi-ball, a wide paddle, or a sweep of one sender. At the end, pick Trash or Archive for each sender.

Is it safe?

  • Nothing changes until you press Apply. At the end you see every decision, grouped, and can untick any group.
  • Apply uses Gmail's own buttons (Delete, Archive, Mark as read, Add star) on exactly the emails you chose. Emails that look like they're from people are never ticked for deletion: you tick them yourself. Meeting notes from Gemini are never deleted or archived. Deleted mail stays in Trash for 30 days, and archived mail stays in All Mail.
  • The game runs only in your Gmail tab. It reads the emails Gmail has already shown you. It has no server and sends your mail nowhere. Your best score and a list of people you write to (used to tell people from companies) are kept in your own browser.
  • The code is open: github.com/atharva-729/google-workspace-games.

Good to know

  • Updating a game: delete the old bookmark and drag the new one.
  • It works in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers, on Gmail in English with inbox tabs (Primary, Promotions, Social, Updates) on or off.
  • It needs a list of emails on screen. If you're reading an email, go back to the inbox first.
